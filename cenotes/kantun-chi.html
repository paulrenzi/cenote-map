Akumal – Playa corridor

Kantun Chi Ecopark

Entry · $500 MXN Hours · 09:00-17:00 Depth · 5m Typical visibility · ~12m

A family-run ecopark on Highway 307 south of Puerto Aventuras bundling four cenotes — an open swim pool plus cavern-roofed grottos — with life vests, easy walkways, and a jungle trail. A gentle, uncrowded stop good for first-timers and kids.
