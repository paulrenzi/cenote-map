Puerto Morelos jungle

Cenote Popol Vuh

Entry · $200 MXN Hours · 09:00-18:00

Open cenote about 35 m across with vivid blue-green water and a zip line that drops you in. On the Puerto Morelos jungle road, less crowded than Verde Lucero next door.
