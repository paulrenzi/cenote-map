Tulum

Sac Actun (Nohoch Nah Chich)

Entry · not posted Hours · 09:00-16:00 Depth · 12m Typical visibility · ~40m

Part of the world's longest surveyed underwater cave system. Access is by guided cavern snorkel or a cave-certified dive only — crystal freshwater, dramatic decorations, and near-silence. Not a casual swim stop; book a licensed guide.
