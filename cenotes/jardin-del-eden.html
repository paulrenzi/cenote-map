Akumal – Playa corridor

Cenote Jardín del Edén (Ponderosa)

Entry · $200 MXN Hours · 09:00-17:00 Depth · 12m Typical visibility · ~30m

Largest of the Azul-cluster cenotes — wide open pool with cliff jumps, halocline at depth, and one of the friendliest cavern dives in the area. Bring snorkel gear.

Activities

  • Swim
  • Snorkel
  • Cliff jump
  • Cavern dive
  • Cave dive (cert)

Skill level: Open to swimmers

Where it is

Cenotes are sacred to the Maya and fragile freshwater systems — skip the sunscreen, take your trash with you, and tip the local crew.
