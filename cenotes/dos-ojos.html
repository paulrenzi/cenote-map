Dos Ojos

Entry · $600 MXN Hours · 08:00-17:00 Depth · 10m Typical visibility · ~50m

Two connected sinkholes feeding a vast underwater cave system. World-class snorkel with light beams in the morning; the cavern dive is legendary.
