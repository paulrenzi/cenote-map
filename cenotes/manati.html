Cenote Manatí

Entry · $200 MXN Hours · 09:00-17:00 Depth · 8m Typical visibility · ~18m

Quieter cousin of Casa Cenote on the same Tankah lagoon system — shaded mangrove channels, occasional manatee sightings (rare), and no crocodile lore to keep crowds away.
