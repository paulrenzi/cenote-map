Parque Tankah

Entry · not posted Hours · 09:00-17:00 Depth · 6m Typical visibility · ~10m

A commercial adventure park at Km 233+400 on Highway 307 bundling several cenotes with ziplines, kayaks, and canoe channels — sold as activity packages rather than a simple entry fee. Convenient and family-friendly; different place from the natural Cenote Tankah despite the shared name.
