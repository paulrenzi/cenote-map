Cenote Ik Kil

Entry · $180 MXN Hours · 09:00-17:00 Depth · 40m Typical visibility · ~8m

The iconic vine-curtain cenote near Chichén Itzá — combine with the ruins for a single long day from Tulum (2.5hr drive). Tour buses arrive 10am–2pm; first or last in beats the crush.
