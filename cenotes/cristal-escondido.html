Cenote Cristal & Escondido

Entry · $250 MXN Hours · 10:00-17:00 Depth · 5m Typical visibility · ~20m

Two open cenotes across the highway from each other — one ticket gets you both. Wooden jump platforms; clear shallow water; locals' favorite for an afternoon dip.

Where it is

Cenotes are sacred to the Maya and fragile freshwater systems — skip the sunscreen, take your trash with you, and tip the local crew.
