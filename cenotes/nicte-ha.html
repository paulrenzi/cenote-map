Cenote Nicte-Ha

Entry · $200 MXN Hours · 09:00-17:00 Depth · 10m Typical visibility · ~30m

A quiet, lily-pad-fringed open cenote near the Dos Ojos entrance — shallow, clear, and usually calm, with a cavern edge for divers. A pretty, low-key swim and snorkel stop that most Dos Ojos day-trippers skip.
