Cenote Zacil-Há

Entry · $200 MXN Hours · 10:00-17:00 Depth · 3m Typical visibility · ~20m

Shallow open-air pool with a zip line over the water — easy family stop. Often combined with neighboring Carwash and Calavera in a half-day loop.
