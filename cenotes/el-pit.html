Cenote El Pit

Entry · not posted Hours · 08:00-17:00 Depth · 40m Typical visibility · ~50m

The deepest cenote in the Dos Ojos / Sac Actun area — a vertical shaft dropping past a hydrogen-sulphide cloud and ancient tree limbs, with legendary morning light beams. A technical dive site for certified divers only; not for swimmers or snorkelers.
