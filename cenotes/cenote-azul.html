Cenote Azul

Entry · $200 MXN Hours · 09:00-17:30 Depth · 7m Typical visibility · ~15m

Iconic open-air cenote between Akumal and Playa — shallow kids' pool, deeper swim area with a small cliff jump, schools of tiny fish that nibble your feet. The most family-friendly stop on Federal 307.
